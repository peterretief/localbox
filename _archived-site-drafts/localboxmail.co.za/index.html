The journey

Start by securing your domain name. LocalBox parks it first, then you can leave it parked, send another provider's nameservers, or ask LocalBox to prepare hosting.

1. Find your address

Start with the name people will remember. Search for a domain, compare the available extensions, and see the registration price before you commit.

  • Search .co.za and other supported extensions
  • Use a free LocalBox subdomain while you explore
  • Ask us about a name that needs a closer look
2. Secure it, then take your time

Once registered in your name, your domain does not need to become a full project overnight. Keep it parked while you prepare your brand, gather content, or decide where you want to host.

  • Keep a simple holding page online
  • Use another DNS provider if you prefer
  • Move to LocalBox hosting when you are ready
3. Choose what it should do

Create your LocalBox account and choose the level of help you need. We can leave the domain parked, or provision the useful starting point: a basic website, a contact form, and your first email address.

  • Complete payment securely
  • Confirm your domain and primary email address
  • Choose LocalBox hosting or provide your own DNS direction
4. Grow into your workspace

Open with something simple, then build as your needs become clearer. Your domain becomes the front door to the services you actually use, rather than a bundle of features you have to configure on day one.

  • Design and publish your website
  • Add mailboxes, aliases, and contact-form delivery
  • Share files and add services as your work grows
